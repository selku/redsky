Welcome to REDSKY OS.

The REDSKY OS was built from scratch on legacy i386 based systems to demostrate modular kernel concept.
The core concept is that hardware components on a system may become plug and play in the future. 
We should be able to plug additional RAM, processor or storage easily at runtime. 
The operating system will construct the system bassed on the available hardware during booting and is expected to adapt to plugged-in new capabilities.

This was my research project during my undergraduate days.
After a very long time, I have found time to look back into this.
